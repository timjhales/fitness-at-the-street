Fitness At The Street — New Member UX Concept

Purpose: Content & Journey Approval

This prototype is intended to confirm that the proposed member journey, services and messaging accurately reflect the Fitness At The Street experience before detailed design and development.

Start here

Why we're proposing a restructure

Today's site is organised by page type

Home · About · Classes · Instructors · Snowling Stones · Take The Tour · Find Us · Member Login — each page covers one topic well, but a first-time visitor has to piece the member journey together themselves.

This concept is organised around the new-member journey

The homepage itself is restructured to answer, in order: Is this gym for me? What do I get as a member? What happens when I join? How do I get started? Everything below is built to answer those four questions before anything else.

How to use this document: look at the live dev site first (ducks-new-build.fitnessatthestreet.pages.dev), then work through this page top to bottom. Every section below carries a reviewer note explaining why it's structured that way, and a confirmation flag anywhere we're not certain we've got the real detail right. Nothing here is final — it's a starting point for a conversation about the logic, not a design.

Once we've talked through the reasoning section by section and agreed the structure together, the next step is a higher-fidelity wireframe prototype, and only after that, the real build.
