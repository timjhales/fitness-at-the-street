Who we are

More than just a gym

★ Read our reviews

A strength-focused, community-driven gym in Brundall on the outskirts of Norwich. Personal training, small group classes, and a community that pushes you further.

We have 3 qualified Personal Trainers on site, a variety of classes and an on-demand library — all built around what our members actually want.
