Who we are

More than just a gym

Fitness at the Street is a community based gym in Brundall on the outskirts of Norwich. We offer all our members both cardio and resistance training, depending on their goals. We take our time to find out what they actually want to achieve, their preferences, niggles — and then provide them with a plan to help them towards that goal.

We take pride in our customer service — you won't find this in any big gym! We have 3 qualified Personal Trainers on site, ready and willing to give you any advice, guidance or just to chat to.

All our gym members have access to our gym during opening hours, plus we offer a variety of classes from gentle fitness, bootcamp, barbells and clubbercise — plus many more. We listen to our members and change the classes every 3 months reflecting their requests and to get them training in different ways. Our classes are face to face, online and on demand, with a library of previous classes to dip in and out of as and when it suits.

Our Personal Trainers are qualified in a variety of training methods and offer online, 1-2-1, and small group sessions. We have helped clients achieve fat loss, strength and muscular gains, and prepare for events including Strongman, 10K, Triathlons, Marathons, Powerlifting competitions and much more.

Most importantly, we will provide you with the support and motivation you need to hit your target — plus have bags of fun along the way. This is your first step in your journey to a fitter you.
